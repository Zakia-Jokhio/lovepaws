Dog Care

Essential tips to keep your canine companion healthy and happy

Nutrition & Feeding

Healthy diet for a happy dog

  • Feed age-appropriate food (puppy, adult, senior)
  • Provide fresh water at all times
  • Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions & xylitol
  • Divide meals into 2–3 servings per day
  • Consult a vet before giving supplements

Health & Vet Visits

Keep your pup in top shape

  • Schedule annual wellness check-ups
  • Keep vaccinations up to date
  • Use monthly flea and tick prevention
  • Deworm every 3–6 months
  • Watch for changes in eating or behaviour

Grooming

Looking great, feeling great

  • Brush coat 2–3 times a week (daily for long hair)
  • Bathe every 4–6 weeks or as needed
  • Trim nails every 2–4 weeks
  • Clean ears weekly to avoid infection
  • Brush teeth several times per week

Exercise & Play

An active dog is a happy dog

  • Aim for at least 30–60 minutes of exercise daily
  • Vary activities: fetch, walks, swimming
  • Provide mental stimulation with puzzle toys
  • Socialise with other dogs regularly
  • Avoid overexercising puppies under 1 year

Cat Care

Keep your feline friend purring with these expert tips

Nutrition & Feeding

Cats are obligate carnivores

  • Feed high-protein, meat-based diet
  • Always provide fresh, clean water
  • Avoid onions, garlic, milk & raw fish
  • Feed 2–3 small meals per day
  • Transition new foods gradually over 7–10 days

Health & Vet Visits

Prevention is better than cure

  • Annual vet check-ups are essential
  • Core vaccines include rabies and FVRCP
  • Spay or neuter by 6 months of age
  • Check for dental disease yearly
  • Monthly flea and parasite prevention

Environment & Enrichment

A stimulating space = a happy cat

  • Provide a clean litter box (1 per cat + 1 extra)
  • Offer cat trees, perches and window views
  • Interactive toys combat boredom
  • Scratching posts protect your furniture
  • Keep safe hiding spots available

Grooming & Hygiene

Cats groom but still need your help

  • Brush short-haired cats weekly; long-haired daily
  • Check and clean ears gently each week
  • Trim nails every 2–3 weeks
  • Brush teeth regularly to prevent tartar
  • Bathe only when absolutely necessary

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon should I take a new pet to the vet? ▼
Within the first week of bringing your pet home. A vet will assess their health, confirm or start vaccinations, check for parasites, and give you a personalised care plan.
What vaccinations does my pet need? ▼
Dogs need Rabies, Distemper, Parvovirus, and Hepatitis (core vaccines). Cats need Rabies and FVRCP (Feline Viral Rhinotracheitis, Calicivirus, Panleukopenia). Your vet will recommend a schedule based on age and lifestyle.
How do I introduce a new pet to my home? ▼
Start by giving them a quiet, small space to adjust. Gradually introduce them to other areas and family members. If you have other pets, do slow, supervised introductions over several days to reduce stress and territorial behaviour.
Is it necessary to neuter/spay my pet? ▼
Highly recommended. Spaying and neutering prevents unwanted litters, reduces the risk of certain cancers and infections, and can improve behaviour. Most vets recommend doing this around 6 months of age.
What should I do in a pet emergency? ▼
Stay calm and contact your nearest emergency vet immediately. Keep the vet's emergency number saved in your phone. In Karachi, several 24-hour veterinary clinics are available. Do not attempt home treatment for serious injuries or illness.
