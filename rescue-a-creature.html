What Happens Next?

  • Our team reviews your report within 30 minutes
  • A rescue volunteer is dispatched to the location
  • Animal receives immediate first aid if needed
  • Moved to a safe shelter for recovery
  • Health checked and vaccinated by our vet
  • Listed for adoption once ready

Tips While You Wait

  • Do not corner or chase an injured animal
  • Offer water from a distance if safe to do so
  • Keep other people and pets away
  • Stay nearby if possible so you can guide the team
  • Take a photo to help the team identify the animal
