Our Story

LovePaws was founded in 2023 by a small group of animal welfare advocates in Karachi who saw too many stray animals living without love or safety. What started as a WhatsApp group sharing rescued animals grew into a fully-fledged adoption platform serving five cities across Pakistan.

We believe every animal deserves a second chance. Our shelter partners, volunteer network, and digital platform work together to make the adoption process simple, transparent, and joyful — for both the pet and the family.

Today, LovePaws is more than a website — it's a community of people united by compassion for animals. From first-time adopters to experienced pet owners, we support every family on their journey.

What We Stand For

Our core values guide everything we do

Compassion First

Every decision we make centres around the wellbeing of the animals in our care.

Trust & Transparency

We are honest about each pet's history, health, and personality so you can adopt confidently.

Community

We build lasting connections between adopters, volunteers, vets, and animal lovers everywhere.

Animal Safety

All pets are health-checked and vaccinated before listing. Their wellbeing is always our top priority.
